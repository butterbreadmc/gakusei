Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

The short version: Gakusei doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your learning progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Gakusei does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Your learning progress

Gakusei saves your progress (completed lessons, your streak, which words, kana and kanji you know, and your settings) on your device only. This data is never sent to us. If your device is backed up to iCloud or your computer, that backup may include Gakusei's data; those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

You can erase your progress at any time in the app: Settings › Reset progress. Deleting the app also deletes its data.

Audio

Japanese audio is produced on your device by your iPhone's built-in speech feature. No text or audio is sent anywhere.

Network access

Gakusei works fully offline. All lessons, fonts and content are included in the app, and the app does not connect to our servers or to third-party services.

Third-party services

Gakusei does not include third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Children

Gakusei is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Gakusei changes how data is handled (for example, by adding optional accounts to sync progress), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
