Learn Japanese, one day at a time

Gakusei (学生, "student") is a complete beginner Japanese course in 100 lessons. No hearts, no ads, no limits.

Download on the App Store

What's inside

  • 100 lessons in 10 units, about 15 minutes each
  • Hiragana and katakana, then 190 of the most important kanji
  • 700+ everyday words and the grammar to use them
  • A conversation at the end of every lesson
  • Review lessons, plus a daily refresh once you finish
  • Works fully offline, and your progress stays on your device
