Common questions

I can't hear any audio

Gakusei uses your iPhone's Japanese voice. Check that your phone isn't on silent and the volume is up. For a clearer voice, go to Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › Japanese and download an enhanced voice.

Why can't I open later lessons?

Lessons unlock in order, so each one builds on the last. Finish the current lesson to open the next. You can replay any lesson you've finished, and Practice is always available.

When do kanji start?

Gakusei teaches kanji once you know all hiragana and katakana. Each lesson warms up with the kana it uses; you can practice the rest in the Kana section. Until then, everything is written in kana.

How do I turn off romaji or furigana?

Tap the gear on the home screen. Inside a lesson, the ro button toggles romaji and the ふ button toggles furigana.

Is my progress backed up?

Your progress is saved on your iPhone and included in your iCloud or computer backups. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I start over?

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Reset progress twice.
